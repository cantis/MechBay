MechBay

A BattleTech miniature inventory management system built with Flask.

MechBay helps BattleTech / Alpha Strike enthusiasts keep track of their miniature collections, organize them into forces and lances, and streamline the process of building and managing their armies. I.e. do I have that miniature, which lance is it in? The intention is to support managing your physical collection of miniatures, as such, we don't include mech version or loadout details... yet.

Features
  • Track your BattleTech miniature collection
  • Organize miniatures into forces and lances
  • Create and apply lance templates for quick force building
  • Save and open inventory (.mechbay) and forces (.mbforce) from the File menu
  • Export forces to Jeff's BT Tools format
  • Search, sort, and filter your inventory
